♡ A SPECIAL LETTER ♡ 17 NOVEMBER

Happy 14th birthday, my dearest Arum! ♡

Today is your special day, and I just want to remind you how wonderful you are. I hope you know how much happiness your presence brings into my life.

Honestly, words will never be enough to describe how grateful I am to have someone like you around. You make ordinary days feel a little more special, and somehow, even the smallest things become beautiful when they involve you.

Fourteen years ago, the world welcomed someone who would eventually become a very special part of my story. And today, I get to celebrate another beautiful year of your existence.

On your birthday, I wish you genuine happiness, endless laughter, good health, and all the beautiful things this world has to offer.

I hope every dream you're working on slowly finds its way to becoming reality. I hope every difficult day makes you stronger, and every little victory reminds you how capable you truly are.

Please never be too hard on yourself. You don't have to have everything figured out. It's okay to take your time, make mistakes, and start over. You're doing better than you think.

I hope you always find reasons to smile, even on days when everything feels a little difficult. Never forget that you deserve kindness, peace, and happiness.

Thank you for being yourself. Thank you for all the little moments, the random conversations, the laughter, and everything that makes our story worth remembering.

If I could wish for one more thing, it would be to see you grow into the happiest version of yourself. I want to see you achieve your dreams, celebrate your success, and enjoy every beautiful chapter of your life.

And please, don't forget to take care of yourself. Eat well, get enough rest, keep smiling, and never lose that wonderful person you are.

You deserve a life filled with kindness, beautiful surprises, sincere people, and countless reasons to be happy.

Once again, happy 14th birthday, Arum! May this new chapter be even more beautiful than the last one.

You deserve all the love and happiness in the universe. I hope you never forget that. ♡

With all my love,

Niel Cakep 🎀
